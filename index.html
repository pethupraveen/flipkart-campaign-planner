Optimizer rules & thresholds

The action plan is rules-based. Tune the thresholds to your margins and risk appetite.
Break-even ROAS ≈ 1 ÷ profit margin (33% margin → 3.0). Rules fire in priority order:
1. PAUSE — meaningful spend & clicks but zero units → price, stock, rating or wrong-keyword problem.
2. CUT BID — ROAS below break-even on meaningful spend → losing money.
3. SCALE — ROAS ≥ target × multiplier → raise bids/budget to buy more volume.
4. REDUCE — profitable but under target → trim bid ~10–15%.
5. FIX CONTENT — big views, tiny CTR → thumbnail/title/price is the bottleneck.

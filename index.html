What do these words mean?
ROASReturn on ad spend — for every ₹1 you spend on ads, how many ₹ you get back in sales.
CPCCost per click — how much you pay Flipkart each time someone clicks your ad.
CTRClick-through rate — out of everyone who saw your ad, what % clicked it. Low = your thumbnail/title isn't grabbing attention.
CVRConversion rate — out of everyone who clicked, what % actually bought. Low = the product page needs work (price, photos, reviews, stock).
AOVAverage order value — the average ₹ amount per sale.
FSNFlipkart's unique ID number for one specific product listing.

The basics

Just 3 questions — we'll work out the rest automatically. Don't worry about getting these perfect; you can change them any week.
33%
Example: if a product sells for ₹500 and costs you ₹335 (product + fees + shipping), your margin is 33%. Below this margin, an ad sale actually loses you money — we use it to tell PAUSE-worthy products apart from healthy ones.
Most sellers aim for ₹4–6 back per ₹1 spent. Higher = more cautious, lower = more willing to spend for growth.
Advanced settings (only change these if you know what they do)
How decisions are made, in order:
1. Turn off — meaningful spend & clicks but zero sales → price, stock, rating or wrong-keyword problem.
2. Spend less — below break-even on meaningful spend → losing money.
3. Spend more — well above your target → raise bids/budget to buy more volume.
4. Trim a little — profitable but under target → trim bid ~10–15%.
5. Fix the listing — lots of views, tiny click rate → thumbnail/title/price is the bottleneck, not the ad.
